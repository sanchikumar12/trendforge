Privacy Policy & Data Safety

Effective: August 20, 2026 • App: com.trendforge.trendforge

Welcome to TrendForge – AI Carousel Maker. We believe in 100% data transparency and user privacy. This policy details how our Android application handles creator information, AI requests, and storage permissions with zero unauthorized tracking.

🔒 Local-First
Zero external cloud user database accounts.
🚫 Zero Ads Tracking
No GAID, GPS, or behavioral advertising tracking.
⚡ Encrypted AI
Ephemeral TLS inference with zero model training.

🔒 1. Local-First Profile Storage

All creator configuration details—including your creator name, social media handle (@handle), professional role, and customized signature palette colors—are stored strictly locally on your Android device via secure SharedPreferences.

Guarantee: TrendForge does not upload or sync your personal identity or brand kit assets to third-party ad brokers or public clouds.

🧠 2. AI Generation & Ephemeral Processing

When you create carousels or repurpose ideas:

  • Your input topics, voice transcripts, or article prompts are sent over encrypted TLS/HTTPS connections to high-speed AI inference endpoints (Groq / Gemini).
  • Inference happens ephemerally to produce the structured 6-slide narrative arc.
  • Your prompt data is never stored, sold, or used to train public foundation models.

📸 3. Media & Storage Permissions

TrendForge requires storage/media permissions exclusively for two actions triggered directly by you:

  • PNG Pack Export: Saving individual high-resolution 1080x1350 px slides to your device gallery.
  • LinkedIn PDF Export: Generating and saving multi-page swipeable document carousels.

The app never scans, reads, or modifies unrelated files or photos in your gallery.

🗑️ 4. Data Retention & Instant Deletion

Because all user data resides locally on your Android device, simply uninstalling or clearing app data in Android Settings instantly and permanently deletes all stored brand kits and local cache files.
