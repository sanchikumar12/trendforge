Privacy Policy

Effective Date: August 20, 2026 • Google Play Store Compliance

TrendForge ("we", "our", or "the App") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy outlines how user information is handled on Android.

🔒 1. Zero Tracking & Local-First Storage

All creator branding preferences (Creator Name, @handle, Role, Theme Colors) are stored strictly locally on your device. We do not maintain user databases, nor do we track advertising identifiers (GAID), GPS location, or personal contacts.

⚡ 2. AI Prompt & Generation Processing

When generating carousel slides or analyzing topics:

  • Prompts and topics are transmitted over secure, encrypted TLS/HTTPS connections to high-speed AI inference endpoints (Groq Cloud / Google AI).
  • Data is processed ephemerally and is never stored or used to train public foundation models.

📸 3. Media & Storage Permissions

TrendForge requires storage permissions strictly to save your exported high-res 1080x1350 PNG slide packs and LinkedIn PDF documents to your local device gallery.

🇮🇳 4. Global & Regional Compliance

Fully compliant with the Google Play Developer Policy, Data Safety standards, and international consumer privacy frameworks.
