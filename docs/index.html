Privacy Policy

📅 Effective Date: August 20, 2026 🛡️ Application: TrendForge – AI Carousel Maker
TrendForge ("we", "our", or "the App") is committed to protecting your privacy and maintaining complete data transparency. This policy outlines how user data is handled with zero data selling or unauthorized tracking.

🔒 1. Local-First Storage (Zero Cloud Profile Storage)

Your Brand Kit details (Creator Name, @handle, Role, Theme Preferences) are stored exclusively on your local device via Android secure preferences. We do not maintain central user databases, nor do we track device advertising identifiers (GAID), GPS location, or personal contacts.

⚡ 2. AI Processing & Generation

When you generate carousel slides or analyze trending topics:

  • Prompts, topics, or voice transcripts are transmitted securely over TLS/HTTPS encryption to our AI endpoints.
  • Data is processed ephemerally solely to generate your 6-slide carousel output.
  • Your input data is never stored, sold, or used to train public AI models.

📸 3. Media & Storage Permissions

TrendForge requests device storage/media access strictly to save exported high-resolution PNG slide packs (1080x1350) and LinkedIn multi-page PDF documents to your device gallery, and to share them via the standard Android Share sheet.

🗑️ 4. Data Retention & Deletion

Because all configuration data resides locally on your Android device, uninstalling the TrendForge application immediately and permanently deletes all stored data.
