# Privacy Policy for TrendForge – AI Carousel Maker

**Effective Date:** August 20, 2026  
**Last Updated:** August 20, 2026  

TrendForge ("we", "our", or "the App") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how your information is handled when you use the TrendForge mobile application on Android.

---

### 1. Information Collection and Storage
* **Local Storage Only:** All creator brand profiles, including your creator name, `@handle`, role, and signature brand colors, are stored locally on your device. We do not maintain user accounts or store personal identities on external database servers.
* **No Tracking / Profiling:** We do not collect device advertising IDs (GAID), GPS location, browsing history, or contacts.

---

### 2. Live AI Processing
When you use AI generation features (such as topic expansion, voice transcription, or article repurposing):
* Your input prompts or transcripts are securely transmitted to our backend AI providers (Groq / Gemini) over encrypted TLS/HTTPS connections.
* Prompts are processed strictly for ephemeral slide generation and are **never used to train public foundation models**.

---

### 3. Media & Storage Permissions
* **Media & Gallery Access:** TrendForge requests storage / media write permissions exclusively to save your generated high-resolution PNG slide packs and LinkedIn PDF documents to your local device.
* The App does not read or upload any unrelated photos or files from your gallery.

---

### 4. Third-Party Services
TrendForge may interact with standard platform services:
* **Google Play Services:** For app delivery and crash analytics.
* **Groq Cloud / Google AI:** For high-speed large language model inference.

---

### 5. Data Retention & Deletion
Because all your data is stored locally on your device, deleting the application from your Android device immediately and permanently removes all stored data.

---

### 6. Children's Privacy
TrendForge does not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13.

---
