# Privacy Policy for TrendForge – AI Carousel Maker

**Effective Date:** August 20, 2026  
**Last Updated:** August 20, 2026  
**Package Name:** `com.trendforge.trendforge`  

TrendForge ("we", "our", or "the App") is committed to protecting your privacy and providing 100% data transparency. This Privacy Policy describes how your information is handled when you use the TrendForge mobile application on Android.

---

### 1. Information Collection and Storage
* **Local Storage Only:** All creator brand profiles, including your creator name, `@handle`, role, and signature brand colors, are stored locally on your device via secure Android SharedPreferences. We do not maintain user accounts or store personal identities on external database servers.
* **No Tracking / Profiling:** We do not collect device advertising IDs (GAID), GPS location, browsing history, or contacts.

---

### 2. Live AI Processing
When you use AI generation features (such as topic expansion, voice transcription, or article repurposing):
* Your input prompts or transcripts are securely transmitted to high-speed AI inference endpoints over encrypted TLS/HTTPS connections.
* Prompts are processed strictly for ephemeral slide generation and are **never stored, sold, or used to train public foundation models**.

---

### 3. Media & Storage Permissions
* **Media & Gallery Access:** TrendForge requests storage / media permissions exclusively to save your generated high-resolution PNG slide packs (1080x1350) and LinkedIn multi-page PDF documents to your local device.
* The App does not read or upload any unrelated photos or files from your gallery.

---

### 4. Data Retention & Deletion
Because all your data is stored locally on your device, deleting the application from your Android device immediately and permanently removes all stored data.
